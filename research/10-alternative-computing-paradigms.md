*Draft — research in progress, 2026-09-07*

## TL;DR (placeholder)

Photonic interconnect is real and shipping; photonic *compute* is not. Thermodynamic/probabilistic and reversible computing are pre-product with enormous claimed numbers. Analog in-memory is shipping at the edge, not the datacenter. Wafer-scale is the only "exotic" paradigm already in production AI datacenters at scale.
